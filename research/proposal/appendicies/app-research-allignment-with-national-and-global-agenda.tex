\section{Alignment with programme specialization}

This research aligns with the Artificial Intelligence and Robotics specialization track of the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science. Reinforcement learning, in which an agent learns to act by interacting with an environment, is a core method of both robotics and artificial intelligence. The project applies deep reinforcement learning, decision-making under partial observability, and safe reinforcement learning, in which a learning agent is kept within explicit constraints, a problem first studied for robots and control systems~\cite{alshiekh2018}.

\section{Alignment with Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) }

The research aligns with SDG 9, Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure, which calls for resilient infrastructure and inclusive innovation. Reliable IT services are part of an institution's infrastructure, and the project studies how automation can support the people who restore those services without acting outside the rules they set. It also supports SDG 4, Quality Education, since the university's teaching, learning and administration depend on the IT services that the Directorate maintains.
